## Film 2 — Your Everyday Life — production script for review
- 0–3s: “Say it out loud.” Show the ChatGPT voice interface on a real device.
- 3–9s: Speak: “Help me draft a message to a friend. I'd like to meet for a walk this weekend. Ask which day suits. Keep it friendly and brief.”
- 9–14s: Show the actual returned draft from the recorded session. Target example: “Fancy a walk this weekend? Let me know which day suits you.” This is a proposed demonstration, not a claim about a recorded output.
- 14–18s: “Check it. Make it yours.” Show the draft being reviewed. Do not show it being sent.
- 18–24s: Shared training end card.
- Supporting caption can mention drafting messages, planning activities and organising tasks; the video itself demonstrates just the message draft. Do not imply calendar or task-app integration.

## Film 3 — Your Business — production script for review
- 0–3s: “Another enquiry to answer?” Show fictional notes, clearly labelled “Example”.
- 3–8s: Notes: “New customer asks about a small job. Need a description and photos before discussing a quote.”
- 8–13s: Prompt: “Draft a friendly reply asking for a short description and photos. Under 90 words. Don't promise a price, start date or availability.”
- 13–18s: Show the actual draft from the recorded session. Visually check “details requested”, “tone” and “promises”. If edited or sped up, label the demonstration as shortened.
- 18–24s: Shared training end card.
- Caption: “Bring a task from your working week. I offer practical ChatGPT training for business owners and small teams, built around the work you want help with. We can cover email replies, marketing drafts and everyday admin. Online across the UK, or in person across Edinburgh and the Lothians. Message me about training or visit bear-media.com/training.”
