## Film 2 — Your Everyday Life

### Record
1. Use a clean ChatGPT conversation and a device signed in to Garry's own account. Hide prior conversation titles and personal notifications.
2. Record a genuine voice interaction. Say: “Help me draft a message to a friend. I'd like to meet for a walk this weekend. Ask which day suits. Keep it friendly and brief.”
3. Capture the actual returned text; don't substitute a made-up result in a ChatGPT screen.
4. Capture reviewing the wording. The demonstration ends with a draft; no message is sent.
5. If the wait is shortened, place “Demonstration shortened” on screen. Don't claim a fixed response time.

### Edit, target 24 seconds
0–3: “Say it out loud.”
3–9: Genuine voice request, with the request readable on screen.
9–14: Actual message draft.
14–18: “Check it. Make it yours.”
18–24: Shared training end card.

## Film 3 — Your Business

### Record
1. Start a clean ChatGPT conversation using only this fictional business example.
2. Show notes: “New customer asks about a small job. Need a description and photos before discussing a quote.”
3. Enter: “Draft a friendly reply to a new customer asking about a small job. Thank them and ask for a short description and photos. Keep it under 90 words. Don't promise a price, start date or availability.”
4. Record the actual generated draft. Keep enough time to read its key lines.
5. Show the owner reviewing the details requested, tone and commitments. No enquiry is sent, no price or time-saving result is claimed.

### Edit, target 24 seconds
0–3: “Another enquiry to answer?”
3–8: Rough enquiry notes labelled “Fictional example”.
8–13: The prompt, with the context and limits highlighted.
13–18: Actual draft; highlight the details and commitments to check.
18–24: Shared training end card.

### Caption for review
Another enquiry to answer? ✉️

Give ChatGPT the background, explain what you need the reply to do and tell it which details it mustn't invent. Then check the draft before you use it.

In this example, the reply asks for more information before discussing a quote. You stay in charge of the price, timing and promises.
